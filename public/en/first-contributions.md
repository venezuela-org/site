# Conexión RedJIV · Your guide to a first contribution

Editorial review: 2026-10-24

Briefs for a first draft, with scope, review criteria and sources. These are educational proposals; confirm priority and review availability with the community before expanding the work.

> This is not an emergency service. For urgent assistance, use your local official channels. Our work is remote and uses public information.

## D · Check three public sources

60 min · Proposed · confirm before expanding

Turn a list of links into a table another person can verify.

**You will deliver:** A 3-source table: institution, URL, purpose, consultation date and limitations.

**You need:** A browser and a spreadsheet or Markdown file.

### Ready for review when…

- Every link leads to the expected institutional domain.
- Each row separates what was checked from what remains uncertain.
- No personal data or unsupported claims about service availability are included.

**Keep it within scope:** Do not contact affected people or test forms with real data. A reachable page does not prove a service is operational.

- [ICRC · Venezuela](https://www.icrc.org/es/donde-trabajamos/venezuela)
- [UNICEF · Venezuela](https://www.unicef.org/venezuela/)
- [Starter template or example](https://github.com/venezuela-org/site/blob/main/examples/source-check/README.md)

## B · Learn to read a mapping task

120 min · Proposed · confirm before expanding

Explore HOT’s workflow and explain how to start without duplicating effort.

**You will deliver:** A 1-page note with a public project link, prerequisites and 3 beginner questions.

**You need:** A browser. This note does not require an account or map editing.

### Ready for review when…

- The project status and consultation date are recorded, without assuming contributions are open.
- Project training and instructions are linked.
- The note explains that later edits must follow HOT’s review process.

**Keep it within scope:** Onboarding documentation only. Do not publish people’s locations, infer aid needs or edit maps before completing required training.

- [HOT · Tasking Manager](https://tasks.hotosm.org/)

## C · Organise safe-water references

60 min · Proposed · confirm before expanding

Help a reviewer find and compare institutional documents.

**You will deliver:** An annotated bibliography of 3 documents: date, audience, scope and link.

**You need:** A browser and a text document.

### Ready for review when…

- Each document has an institutional author, title, date or unknown-date note, and URL.
- Annotations distinguish general context from technical instructions.
- The draft explicitly states that it is not guidance for field use.

**Keep it within scope:** Do not recommend doses, treatments or procedures. Do not adapt health instructions for distribution; practical materials require specialist review.

- [ICRC · Venezuela](https://www.icrc.org/es/donde-trabajamos/venezuela)
- [UNICEF · Venezuela](https://www.unicef.org/venezuela/)

## A · Review a page using your keyboard

60 min · Proposed · confirm before expanding

Find one concrete barrier for someone getting started.

**You will deliver:** A report with the page, reproduction steps, expected result and observed result.

**You need:** A browser and a keyboard. No paid tools required.

### Ready for review when…

- The path can be repeated with Tab, Shift+Tab and Enter.
- Visible focus, descriptive links and use at 200% zoom are checked.
- One concrete observation is documented without claiming a full audit.

**Keep it within scope:** Review public content only. Do not capture names, private messages or other people’s data.



## D · Reproduce a table with synthetic data

120 min · Proposed · confirm before expanding

Practise a small analysis without handling data about people.

**You will deliver:** A change to the 5-row synthetic example, with expected and observed totals, a manual check and a run command.

**You need:** Python 3 or JavaScript with Node.js, and a text editor.

### Ready for review when…

- Rows are labelled synthetic and contain no names, contacts or locations.
- One command reproduces the result and the runtime version is documented.
- Totals are checked by hand and the example’s limits are explained.

**Keep it within scope:** A reproducibility exercise, not an assessment of real needs. Do not collect or scrape victim or patient data.


- [Starter template or example](https://github.com/venezuela-org/site/blob/main/examples/synthetic-table/README.md)

## A · Make an explanation clearer

60 min · Proposed · confirm before expanding

Help someone understand the next step without knowing the jargon.

**You will deliver:** A 150–250-word before/after proposal and a 5-term glossary, in Spanish or English.

**You need:** A text editor and a public section of this repository.

### Ready for review when…

- The proposal preserves meaning without adding facts or promises.
- Five terms are explained and the original text is linked.
- Translation or content questions are marked for review.

**Keep it within scope:** Do not translate clinical or safety instructions for operational use. Choose onboarding or collaboration documentation.



## When sharing your draft

- A summary of what you did and who could use it.
- The file or link, without personal data.
- Sources and consultation dates.
- Steps to check or reproduce the result.
- Limitations, questions and any AI assistance used.

## A message to get started

Hi, I am a student and would like to contribute to: [task name].
I can offer [hours per week] for [period].
My first draft would be: [deliverable].
Is this task still useful? Is someone available to review it?
I will use public information only, without personal data.
